Dice Roller: Tap to Roll - Support

A clean, fast dice roller for board games and tabletop RPGs.

How to use

  • Tap Roll to roll the dice.
  • Pick a dice type from d4 to d20.
  • Choose how many dice to roll (1 to 5).
  • See the instant total and your recent roll history.
